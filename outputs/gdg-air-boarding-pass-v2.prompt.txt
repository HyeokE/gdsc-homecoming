Use case: product-mockup
Create a faithful-looking AIRPORT CHECK-IN COUNTER PRINTED PAPER BOARDING PASS for the fictional event airline GDG AIR. The previous design failed because it looked like a souvenir poster with huge ICN/NRT typography and a luxurious black banner. This is a NEW design, completely abandoning those proportions.

Visual target: the mundane authentic utilitarian boarding card handed out at an international airline check-in desk: off-white thin card stock, dense black dot-matrix/thermal type in compact aligned rows, small gray bilingual field labels, understated preprinted airline identity, dominant white paper, practical information rather than decorative graphics. Similar information density and typographic proportions to actual Korean airline paper boarding cards, with original GDG branding and no real airline branding or passenger data.

Output one flat straight-on horizontal paper ticket, aspect ratio about 3:1, very small light gray margin around ticket, whole ticket visible, no tilt, no hands/passport/tabletop props. Main coupon 72%, tear-off passenger coupon 28%, vertical microperforated divider, square paper edges, no large circular ticket cutouts. Header is white, NOT a black band. Small multicolor developer chevrons and "GDG AIR" upper left; "탑승권 BOARDING PASS" modest upper right of main coupon. Only small blue red yellow green strokes next to logo, no rainbow bottom stripe.

At lower left a wide short rectangular densely stacked PDF417-style decorative barcode (NOT a one-dimensional supermarket barcode, NOT a QR code). Next to barcode small "GDG HOME COMING" and "21 NOV 2026".
Very bottom tiny gray "EVENT SOUVENIR / NOT VALID FOR AIR TRAVEL".
