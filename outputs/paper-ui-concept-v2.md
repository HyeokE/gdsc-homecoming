```text
Create a substantially more sophisticated second design proposal for a Korean GDG homecoming mobile event app. Output two complete mobile screen designs side by side, each 390:844 aspect ratio, large and straight front view on a neutral gray presentation canvas, no device frames, no perspective. Reference 1 is the REJECTED earlier proposal: it is too plain, the typography is clumsy and the heading resembles a sports jersey stencil. Do NOT reproduce that visual treatment. Reference 2 is the APPROVED boarding ticket: preserve its restrained printed-paper and dark-ink concept, tiny four-color developer mark, functional print alignment. Goal: Swiss airline poster meets contemporary printed invitation, crafted by an excellent editorial digital designer. Visually confident and refined, not merely another monochrome form. One cohesive light-paper system across both screens. Near-white paper #faf9f6, ink #20252a, muted graphite captions, NO beige, blue buttons, black hero, photos, flip panels, random stamps, fake serial numbers, chunky bevels, noisy texture, gradients, shadows on text, sports fonts, pixel fonts or stencils. Large clean condensed neo-grotesk display headline, not monospace for the main headline. Monospace only for small flight/date metadata. Give BOTH screens strong typographic hierarchy with a very large main heading, generous deliberate negative space, and tiny well-aligned supporting metadata. Details should feel architectural and precise. TOP shared masthead: tiny original four-color developer chevrons, small 'GDGoC DJU' wordmark; opposite small 'HOMECOMING 2026'. A fine horizontal rule below. LEFT landing: beautiful tall condensed grotesk title 'HOME' then 'COMING' at roughly 72px in a 390px-wide screen, tight leading, clean straight stems, medium-to-bold weight, aligned left. This is the dominant hero with a strong silhouette. Immediately below in 16px Korean sans: '다시 만나는 우리,' then '이번엔 어디로 떠날까요?' Plenty of space. Supporting text 13px '나만의 여행지를 뽑고, 홈커밍 탑승권을 받아보세요.' Below, introduce a single precisely executed boarding-ticket-inspired perforated horizontal separator with small semicircle edge cutouts, dividing the invitation from the functional itinerary. No boxed card. Itinerary: two-column grid, small labels '일시' and '장소'. Date value in compact neat mono '2026.11.21 15:00' then '~ 11.22 04:00'. Venue underlined '성수 그린힐드' with tiny external arrow. The information is quiet but legible. Near bottom, one black rectangular full-width 52px high action with ivory '발권하기' aligned left and a thin arrow at far right. Gray helper below: '참가 신청에 사용한 이메일을 준비해주세요.' and '발급받은 탑승권도 다시 확인할 수 있어요.' Bottom tiny underlined '문의하기'. RIGHT email page: same shared masthead. Small plain '← 뒤로 가기' link. Muted supporting text '참가 신청에 사용한 이메일로 발권할 수 있어요.' A generous empty breathing interval, then small field label '신청한 이메일', and a beautifully restrained bottom-rule-only input on paper with mono placeholder 'name@example.com', NO standard chunky input box. Black full-width action '확인하기' with thin arrow, same height and alignment as left screen's button. Keep visible helpful whitespace below. Use one subtle perforation detail aligned with the left screen as a visual connection; no excess decoration. Bottom '문의하기'. Make the design feel premium, editorial and memorable through type scale, alignment, rhythm and precise material details, rather than adding features. Korean text must be correct, legible, not overly bold. Outside the screens use small neutral captions '입장' and '참가 확인'. This is a visual direction proposal, not an implementation screenshot.
```
